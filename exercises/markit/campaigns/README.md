# Campaigns

Campaign briefs, plans, and calendars for Planerio.

## Subfolders

- **q1-launch/** — Product launch campaign (Q1 2026). All briefs, plans, and assets for the initial go-to-market push.
- **q2-growth/** — Growth campaign (Q2 2026). Expanding reach after launch, referral programs, upsell campaigns.
- **templates/** — Reusable campaign brief and planning templates for future campaigns.

## What Goes Here

- Campaign briefs
- Campaign plans and timelines
- Content calendars
- Campaign performance summaries

## Naming Convention

`[campaign-name]-[doc-type].md`
Example: `planerio-launch-brief.md`, `q1-content-calendar.md`
