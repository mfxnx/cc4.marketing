# Persona: Director Dana

> "I have 12 people on my team and zero visibility into whether they're actually productive or just busy."

**Type:** Tertiary Persona
**Last Updated:** March 2026

---

## Demographics

| | |
|--|--|
| **Age** | 40–50 |
| **Gender** | Female (representative) |
| **Location** | US-based, hybrid work environment |
| **Education** | BA/MBA in Marketing, Business, or Communications |
| **Income** | $140,000–$190,000/year |
| **Family** | Married, 2–3 kids, active family life |

---

## Role & Responsibilities

- **Title:** Director of Marketing / VP of Marketing
- **Company:** Mid-size company, 200–500 employees, hybrid or remote
- **Team size:** 8–15 people across content, design, demand gen, and ops
- **Reports to:** CMO or CEO

**Day-to-day responsibilities:**
- Setting marketing strategy and OKRs
- Managing campaigns across multiple channels
- Cross-functional coordination with sales, product, and leadership
- Budget planning and vendor management
- Reviewing and approving content
- Reporting on marketing performance to leadership

---

## Pain Points & Frustrations

1. **Cross-team coordination is a mess** — Getting marketing, sales, and product aligned on timelines and priorities requires constant back-and-forth.
2. **Team is always "busy" but output is unclear** — She can see people are in meetings all day but can't tell if actual work is getting done.
3. **Productivity reporting is manual** — She has to ask her team for status updates. There's no system that shows her where time is going.
4. **Too many stakeholders, too many meetings** — Her calendar is dominated by alignment meetings that go in circles.
5. **Campaign deadlines slip** — Without visibility into team capacity and focus time, projects constantly miss their timelines.

> "I can see my team's calendar is full. What I can't see is whether all those hours are producing anything."

| Weekly standups | Team status updates | Time-consuming, often surface-level |

---

## Motivations & Goals

**Professional goals:**
- Hit marketing OKRs without burning out her team
- Prove marketing's impact to the C-suite with clear data
- Build a team that can execute independently without constant oversight

**Personal goals:**
- Leave work at a reasonable hour and be present at home
- Stop being the bottleneck for her team's progress
- Be known as a great leader, not just a demanding boss

**What success looks like to her:**
> "I have a clear view of what my team is working on and when they're in deep work. Projects hit their deadlines. I'm in fewer meetings. My team trusts me to protect their time."
