# Planerio Brand Guidelines

## Brand Voice

Planerio's voice is **professional but friendly** — we speak like a knowledgeable colleague, not a corporate manual. We understand the real challenges of managing remote teams, and we communicate with empathy and confidence.

**Core voice attributes:**
- **Professional but friendly:** We take work seriously without being stiff
- **Knowledgeable but not condescending:** We share expertise without talking down to our audience
- **Direct but warm:** We get to the point while keeping a human tone
- **Confident but humble:** We believe in our product without over-promising

---

## Key Messaging Pillars

### 1. Team Coordination Over Individual Productivity
Planerio isn't just about one person's focus — it's about the entire team working in sync.

> "When your team's focus time aligns, everyone moves faster."

### 2. Reduce Meeting Overload
Deep work gets interrupted by unnecessary meetings. Planerio protects your team's most productive hours.

> "Less interruptions. More output."

### 3. Visibility Without Micromanagement
Managers get clarity on team availability and focus time without hovering over their people.

> "Know what your team is working on — without asking."

### 4. Built for Remote Teams
Remote work has unique coordination challenges. Planerio is designed specifically for distributed teams.

> "Finally, a productivity tool that understands remote teams."

## Tone Guidelines

### Do This
- Use **"we" and "you"** to create a conversational, collaborative feel
- Lead with **benefits before features** — explain the outcome first
- Use **active voice** — "Planerio coordinates your team's focus time"
- Be **specific** — use real numbers and concrete examples when possible
- **Acknowledge the problem** before presenting the solution

### Don't Do This
- Don't use passive voice — avoid "focus time is coordinated by Planerio"
- Don't exaggerate — avoid words like "revolutionary," "game-changing," "best ever"
- Don't be preachy — don't lecture managers on how they should work
- Don't use filler phrases — avoid "cutting-edge," "next-level," "best-in-class"
- Don't make assumptions about team size or structure

### Tone by Channel

| Channel | Tone |
|--------|------|
| Website | Professional, benefit-focused, clear |
| Email | Conversational, helpful, direct |
| LinkedIn | Thoughtful, data-driven, professional |
| Twitter/X | Punchy, relatable, slightly informal |
| Ads | Concise, benefit-first, action-oriented |

## Words to Use

| Use This | Because |
|----------|---------|
| Coordinate | Core to our product's purpose |
| Focus time / Deep work | Speaks directly to user needs |
| Your team | Emphasizes the collective benefit |
| Clarity | Speaks to manager pain points |
| Protect | Conveys value of guarding focus time |
| Sync | Modern, relatable for remote teams |
| Visibility | Key manager need without micromanagement |
| Streamline | Implies efficiency without jargon |

## Words to Avoid

| Avoid This | Use Instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| Revolutionary | Effective / Practical |
| Game-changing | Impactful |
| Cutting-edge | Modern / Smart |
| Leverage | Use |
| Synergy | Collaboration / Coordination |
| Best-in-class | High-performing |
| Disruptive | Different / Better |
| Utilize | Use |
| Robust | Reliable / Powerful |
| Seamless | Smooth / Easy |

## Writing Examples

### Weak (avoid)
> "Our revolutionary, best-in-class platform leverages cutting-edge AI to synergize team productivity at scale."

### Strong (use this)
> "Planerio helps your remote team protect focus time and coordinate deep work — so everyone stays in flow, together."

## Quick Reference

- **Perspective:** We / You
- **Voice:** Confident, warm, direct
- **Length:** Short sentences. Clear paragraphs. No fluff.
- **Goal:** Help managers picture a better way to run their team
