# Research

Market research, competitive analysis, and customer insights for Planerio.

## Subfolders

- **competitors/** — Competitor profiles and analysis (RescueTime, Freedom, Focus@Will, and others)
- **market-data/** — Industry reports, market size data, trends in remote work and productivity
- **customer-insights/** — Customer interviews, survey results, feedback analysis

## What Goes Here

- Competitive positioning documents
- SWOT analyses
- Market opportunity assessments
- Customer pain point summaries
- Analyst reports and industry data

## Naming Convention

`[subject]-[doc-type]-[date].md`
Example: `rescuetime-competitor-analysis-2026.md`, `remote-work-market-data-q1.md`
