## Financial Sector Conduct Authority of South Africa (FSCA)

The **Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA)** is South Africa’s official regulator for market conduct in the financial sector. It oversees companies that provide financial services and products, ensuring they comply with regulations, act transparently, and protect consumer interests.

The FSCA also investigates complaints related to **misconduct or regulatory breaches**, helping uphold fairness and accountability in the financial market.

- License Number: 54594

## The Australian Securities & Investments Commission (ASIC)

The Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) is the integrated corporate, markets, financial services, and consumer credit regulator. ASIC is an independent Australian Government body set up under and administers the Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001 (ASIC Act). OPO FINANCE PTY LTD is regulated by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC).

- License Number: (AFSL) 402043
