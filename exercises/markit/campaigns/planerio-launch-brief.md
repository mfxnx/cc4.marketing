# Campaign Brief: Planerio Product Launch

**Client:** Planerio
**Agency:** Markit
**Campaign:** Product Launch
**Date:** February 2026
**Status:** Draft

---

## Campaign Objectives

1. **Generate awareness** — Reach 50,000 remote team managers in the first 60 days
2. **Drive trial signups** — Acquire 2,000 free trial signups in the first 90 days
3. **Build credibility** — Establish Planerio as the go-to productivity tool for remote teams
4. **Convert trials to paid** — Achieve a 25% trial-to-paid conversion rate

---

## Target Audience

### Primary: Manager Mark
- **Who:** Remote team manager, 35–45 years old
- **Company size:** 50–500 employees, tech industry
- **Pain points:** Too many meetings interrupting deep work, no visibility into team focus time, hard to coordinate schedules across time zones
- **Goals:** Help his team ship faster with fewer interruptions
- **Where he is:** LinkedIn, Hacker News, productivity newsletters, Slack communities

### Secondary: Founder Fiona
- **Who:** Startup founder, 28–35, wearing many hats
- **Company size:** 10–50 employees, early-stage
- **Pain points:** Team is scattered, no system for protecting deep work, meetings eat the day
- **Goals:** Build a high-output culture from the start
- **Where she is:** Twitter/X, Product Hunt, indie hacker communities

### Tertiary: Director Dana
- **Who:** Marketing or ops director, 40–50, mid-size company
- **Company size:** 200–500 employees
- **Pain points:** Cross-team coordination is a mess, productivity reporting is manual
- **Goals:** Improve team output without micromanaging
- **Where she is:** LinkedIn, industry blogs, webinars

## Channel Strategy

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–2)
**Goal:** Build anticipation and an early adopter list

- **Email:** Waitlist nurture sequence (3 emails) to warm up early signups
- **LinkedIn:** Thought leadership posts on remote team productivity (3x/week)
- **Twitter/X:** Teaser content, engage with productivity communities
- **Product Hunt:** Prepare launch page, recruit upvoters

### Phase 2: Launch Week (Weeks 3–4)
**Goal:** Maximum visibility and trial signups

- **Product Hunt:** Launch day post — drive community votes and comments
- **LinkedIn Ads:** Targeted ads to remote team managers at tech companies
- **Email:** Launch announcement to waitlist + press outreach
- **Content:** Publish hero blog post — "Why Remote Teams Struggle with Focus (And How to Fix It)"
- **Twitter/X:** Launch day thread, engage with replies

### Phase 3: Post-Launch Growth (Weeks 5–12)
**Goal:** Sustain momentum and convert trials to paid

- **SEO content:** 2 blog posts/month targeting high-intent keywords
- **LinkedIn:** Case study content, manager tips, product updates
- **Email:** Trial onboarding sequence (5 emails over 14 days)
- **Retargeting ads:** Re-engage website visitors who didn't sign up
- **Referral:** Launch referral program — 1 free month per referred signup
