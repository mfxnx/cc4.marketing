> *Ideal length: 150–300 words. Hook in line 1 — it's all that shows before "see more".*
> *Post frequency: 3–4x per week*

---

**LINKEDIN EXAMPLE**

Remote teams lose an average of 31 hours per month to unproductive meetings.

That's almost a full work week — gone.

And the problem isn't the meetings themselves. It's the lack of a system to protect focus time around them.

Here's what high-output remote teams do differently:

1. They schedule collaboration *after* deep work — not before
2. They set team-wide focus blocks and protect them on the calendar
3. They use async updates to replace status meetings

The result? More output, fewer interruptions, happier team.

What's one thing you do to protect your team's focus time?

#RemoteWork #DeepWork #TeamProductivity #EngineeringLeadership

## Social Post Checklist

### Before Publishing
- [ ] Hook is in the first line (before "see more" on LinkedIn)
- [ ] One clear message — not three ideas crammed together
- [ ] Reads well out loud — natural, not corporate
- [ ] CTA is specific ("Reply with your answer" not "Engage with us")
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant (not stuffed)
- [ ] Image/video attached if needed (LinkedIn posts with images get 2x engagement)

### Brand Voice Check
- [ ] Active voice
- [ ] No buzzwords (seamless, leverage, revolutionary)
- [ ] Sounds like a person, not a brand account
- [ ] Benefit or insight is clear — not just promotional

### Platform-Specific
- [ ] LinkedIn: Under 300 words, professional angle, no more than 5 hashtags
- [ ] Twitter/X: Under 280 chars (single) or tight thread format
- [ ] Threads: Casual, conversational, no heavy formatting
