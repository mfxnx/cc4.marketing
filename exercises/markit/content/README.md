# Content

All generated marketing content for Planerio.

## Subfolders

- **blog/** — Long-form blog posts and article outlines
- **email/** — Email sequences, newsletters, and one-off sends
- **social/** — Social media posts for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and other platforms
- **ads/** — Paid ad copy for LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads, retargeting

## What Goes Here

- Finished and draft content ready for publishing
- Content variations for A/B testing
- Repurposed content across formats

## Naming Convention

`[topic]-[format]-[version].md`
Example: `remote-teams-focus-blog-v1.md`, `launch-linkedin-ad-v2.md`
