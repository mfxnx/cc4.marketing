## Notes on Subject Line Approach

All subject lines follow the brand voice guidelines:

- **Under 50 characters** — optimized for mobile preview
- **No buzzwords** — no "revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless"
- **Lead with the reader** — pain point or benefit first, product second
- **Specific over vague** — "6 hours" not "wasted time"
- **Active voice** — direct and clear

**A/B test recommendation:** Run Subject A vs. Subject B for Email 1 and Email 2. Use the winner's pattern for future sends.
