## Product Overview

**One-liner:** FXNX is an AI-driven forex and CFD broker that gives every trader institutional-grade tools, raw spreads, and a built-in AI co-pilot — in a single account.

**What it does:** FXNX provides access to FX and CFD trading through MT5, with a single universal account (NX One) that includes raw spreads from 0.0 pips, sub-100ms execution, and up to 1:500 leverage. Beyond the trading account, fxnx offers a full ecosystem of AI-powered tools — NX AI (trading co-pilot), NX Learn (education), NX Copy (copy trading), NX Charts (TradingView-powered), NX Gold Vault (gold savings), and NX Miles (loyalty/commission reduction).

**Product category:** Forex broker / CFD broker / AI trading platform

**Product type:** Financial services platform (retail, online)

**Business model:**
- One account type: NX One
- Minimum deposit: $100
- Commission: $9/lot (reduces to $1/lot at Elite NX Miles tier)
- Spreads from 0.0 pips
- No account tiers or hidden fees

**Regulation:** MISA — Mwali International Services Authority, License BFX2025082
**Incorporation:** Saint Lucia (Reg. 2025-00128)
**Restricted jurisdictions:** US, Japan, Canada, Australia, China, Myanmar, North Korea

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem traders face before finding fxnx:**
High spreads and commissions eating into profits, slow or unreliable withdrawals, poor support, and a lack of tools beyond the basic MT5 platform.

**Why current solutions fall short:**
- High spreads at most retail brokers make scalping and high-frequency strategies unprofitable
- Withdrawal delays (days, not hours) destroy trust and convenience
- Poor support — slow response, no personal manager
- No AI tools — traders are left to figure out risk, signals, and market context alone
- Confusing account tiers force traders to choose a product before they understand what they need

**What it costs them:** Lost profits from spread markups, wasted time on manual analysis, frustration from slow withdrawals, anxiety about broker trustworthiness

**Emotional tension:** "Is this broker going to let me withdraw my money?" / "Am I paying too much in fees?" / "I don't know when to enter or exit."

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
1. **NX AI** — a real AI co-pilot with live MT5 access, voice input in 5 languages, signals, risk tools, and account control. No other retail broker has this.
2. **NX Miles** — loyalty program that reduces commission from $9 to $1/lot as traders grow. Rewards volume without requiring tier upgrades.
3. **Single account** — no confusing account tiers. Every trader gets the same premium product from day one.
4. **Full ecosystem in one platform** — trading + AI + education + copy trading + gold savings + charts. Competitors require multiple tools from multiple providers.
5. **Multilingual and MENA-native** — Persian, Arabic, RTL support built in. Not an afterthought.
6. **Islamic/Swap-Free** — available on the same account, no separate product required.

**Why customers choose fxnx:**
Raw spreads, fast execution, and AI tools they can't find at other brokers.

**How fxnx solves it differently:**
Instead of competing only on spreads (a race to the bottom), fxnx adds a full intelligence layer on top of trading — making the platform smarter and more useful the more you trade.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push (away from current broker):**
- Paying too much in spreads and commissions
- Slow or delayed withdrawals
- No tools beyond basic MT5
- Feeling like just another account number

**Pull (toward fxnx):**
- Raw spreads + AI tools at no extra cost
- Commission that actually decreases as you trade more
- A platform that feels built for their region (language, payment methods)

**Habit (keeps them stuck):**
- Already familiar with their current platform
- Don't want to go through KYC again
- Fear of losing open positions during migration

**Anxiety (about switching to fxnx):**
- "Will I be able to withdraw?"
- "Is this broker regulated enough?"
- "What if fxnx disappears?"
