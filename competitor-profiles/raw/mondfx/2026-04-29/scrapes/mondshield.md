## What It Is
Mondshield is a broker-funded loss protection program (not third-party insurance).
Positioning: "loss coverage for a confident start"

## How It Works
- Loss compensation triggered after minimum 10% loss relative to initial deposit
- Compensation calculated as percentage of initial balance
- Account recovery stages: Rebuild, Second Life
- 24/7 dedicated support and consulting

## Eligibility
- Minimum deposit required: $2,000
- Minimum loss to claim: 10% of initial deposit
- Upgrade eligibility: at least 10% profit AND 30+ days since first trade
- One compensation request per stage permitted
