## Roles & Responsibilities

### Marketing Strategist (Markit Lead)
**Overall campaign owner.**

- Write and maintain all campaign documents (brief, timeline, measurement plan)
- Set weekly priorities and unblock the team
- Lead client status meetings
- Final review of all content before publication
- Monitor campaign performance and make optimisation decisions

**Key deliverables:**
- Campaign brief approved by March 28
- Weekly status report every Friday
- Mid-campaign review presentation (May 31)
- Q2 final report (July 7)

---

### Account Manager
**Client relationship and project coordination.**

- Primary point of contact for Planerio day-to-day
- Manage project timeline and flag risks early
- Coordinate approvals and asset delivery
- Ensure client feedback is incorporated within 48 hours

**Key deliverables:**
- Kick-off call agenda and notes (April 1)
- Weekly client update email (Mondays)
- Approval tracking log (maintained throughout)

---

### Content Writer
**All written content.**

- Write 4 blog posts (one per month + one bonus)
- Write email sequences (conversion + referral + webinar follow-up)
- Write LinkedIn post copy (3x per week)
- Write webinar script and speaker notes
- Write ad copy variations

**Key deliverables:**
- Blog post 1 draft: April 4 (publish April 7)
- Trial conversion email sequence: March 31 (live April 1)
- Blog posts 2–4: May 4, May 18, June 1
- Webinar script: May 8

---

### Paid Media Manager
**LinkedIn Ads and retargeting.**

- Set up Q2 LinkedIn ad campaigns (Director Dana audience)
- Build retargeting audiences from Q1 website traffic
- Monitor daily ad spend and performance
- Run creative A/B tests — report results weekly
- Optimise bids and budget allocation monthly

**Key deliverables:**
- LinkedIn Ads live: May 1
- Week 1 performance report: May 8
- Creative refresh: June 8
- Final ad performance report: June 30

---

### Email Specialist
**Email strategy, setup, and execution.**

- Set up trial conversion sequence in email platform
- Segment Q1 trial list (activated vs. not activated)
- Set up referral program emails
- Monitor open rates, click rates, and conversion
- A/B test subject lines

**Key deliverables:**
- Trial conversion sequence live: April 1
- Referral launch email: May 1
- Webinar follow-up sequence: May 16
- A/B test results report: June 1

---

### Design

### Planerio Client Marketing Lead
**Client-side review, approvals, and data.**

- Attend weekly status calls (30 min, Mondays)
