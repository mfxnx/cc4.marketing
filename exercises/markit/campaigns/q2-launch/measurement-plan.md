## Tracking Setup

### UTM Parameter Structure

All campaign links must use UTM parameters for accurate attribution.

**Format:**
`?utm_source=[source]&utm_medium=[medium]&utm_campaign=q2-launch&utm_content=[content-id]`

| Channel | Source | Medium | Example |
|---------|--------|--------|---------|
| LinkedIn Ads | linkedin | paid-social | `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=paid-social` |
| Email | email | email | `utm_source=email&utm_medium=email` |
| LinkedIn organic | linkedin | organic-social | `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic-social` |
| Twitter/X | twitter | organic-social | `utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=organic-social` |
| Blog | blog | content | `utm_source=blog&utm_medium=content` |
| Referral | referral | referral | `utm_source=referral&utm_medium=referral` |
| Webinar | webinar | event | `utm_source=webinar&utm_medium=event` |

## A/B Tests Planned

| Test | Variable | Hypothesis | Timeline |
|------|----------|-----------|---------|
| Email 1 subject line | "Your team lost 6 hours" vs. "Planerio is waiting for you" | Pain-point subject will outperform product-name subject | April 1–7 |
| LinkedIn ad creative | Pain point visual vs. Dashboard screenshot | Dashboard will drive more clicks from Mark; pain point from Dana | May 1–14 |
| CTA copy | "Start Free Trial" vs. "Try It Free" vs. "Get Started Free" | "Try It Free" will outperform (lower friction language) | May 1–31 |
| Email 5 offer | 20% discount vs. free extra month | Free month will outperform (tangible over percentage) | April 22–28 |
