> "I'm trying to build a high-output culture from day one, but I'm also doing the work of five people."

**Last Updated:** March 2026

## Role & Responsibilities

- **Title:** Co-Founder & CEO (or CTO)
- **Company:** Early-stage startup, 5–30 employees, fully remote
- **Team size:** 5–15 people across multiple functions
- **Reports to:** Board / investors (and herself)

**Day-to-day responsibilities:**
- Product strategy and roadmap decisions
- Hiring and building team culture
- Customer calls and sales
- Investor relations and fundraising
- Handling whatever is on fire today
- Writing, designing, coding — whatever needs to get done

---

## Pain Points & Frustrations

1. **Wearing too many hats** — She's the CEO, head of product, chief recruiter, and sometimes the support team. There's never enough time.
2. **Team rhythm is broken** — Her small team is scattered across time zones, and there's no natural cadence to their work.
3. **Async chaos** — She wants async-first culture but has no system to make it actually work.
4. **She can't afford wasted hours** — At this stage, every hour matters. Unproductive meetings are existential.
5. **Culture anxiety** — She worries the team doesn't feel connected or aligned, especially without an office.

> "I want async-first culture, but without a system, it just means everyone is working at random times and nobody knows what anyone else is doing."

---

## Current Solutions

| Tool | How She Uses It | What's Missing |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| Notion | Company wiki, project docs, async updates | Not built for scheduling or coordination |
| Slack | Everything — comms, culture, quick decisions | Interruptions, no focus enforcement |
| Google Calendar | Personal scheduling | No team-wide coordination layer |
| Loom | Async video updates | One-way — doesn't solve coordination |
| Linear | Engineering task tracking | No focus time visibility |

## Motivations & Goals

**Professional goals:**
- Build a company culture that attracts and retains great people
- Ship fast without burning out her small team
- Prove to investors that the team is high-output and well-managed

**Personal goals:**
- Feel less overwhelmed and reactive
- Have evenings and weekends back
- Build something she's proud of

**What success looks like to her:**
> "My team has a rhythm. Everyone knows when to work heads-down and when to collaborate. I can see we're making progress without being in everyone's business."
