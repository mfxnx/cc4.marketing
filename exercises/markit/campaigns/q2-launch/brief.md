# Campaign Brief: Planerio Q2 Growth Campaign

**Client:** Planerio
**Agency:** Markit
**Campaign:** Q2 Growth — Expand and Convert
**Period:** April – June 2026
**Status:** Draft
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Campaign Background

Q1 launched Planerio to market and hit its trial signup targets. Q2 shifts focus from awareness to growth — converting trial users to paid customers, expanding into new segments, and building a referral engine.

**What Q1 established:**
- 2,000 trial signups acquired
- Brand presence on LinkedIn and Twitter/X
- Core messaging validated: "coordinate focus time" resonates strongly with Manager Mark
- Hero blog post performing well organically

**What Q2 builds on:**
- Trial-to-paid conversion (target: 500 paid customers from Q1 trials)
- Expansion into Director Dana segment (enterprise and mid-market)
- Referral program launch to lower CAC
- SEO momentum from Q1 content

## Campaign Objectives

| # | Objective | Metric | Target | Timeframe |

| 1 | Convert Q1 trial users to paid | Trial-to-paid rate | 25% (500 customers) | April 30 |

| 3 | Launch referral program | Referral signups | 500 | End of Q2 |

| 5 | Expand enterprise segment | Demo requests from 200+ headcount companies | 50 | End of Q2 |

---

## Target Audience

### Primary: Existing Trial Users (Convert)
- Q1 trial signups who haven't upgraded
- Pain: haven't experienced full value yet, or haven't activated the team
- Goal: show them the ROI and make upgrading easy

### Secondary: Manager Mark (New Acquisition)
- Same profile as Q1 — remote engineering managers
- Now with social proof and case studies to support outreach
- Channels: LinkedIn, Hacker News, SEO

### Tertiary: Director Dana (New Segment Push)
- Marketing/Ops directors at 200–500 person companies
- New Q2 focus — less explored in Q1
- Channels: LinkedIn Ads, webinars, G2 reviews, demo outreach

## Channel Strategy

### April — Convert
- Email: 5-part trial conversion sequence to Q1 signups
- In-app: upgrade prompts at key activation moments
- LinkedIn: case study content and customer stories

### May — Expand
- LinkedIn Ads: Director Dana segment (retargeting + new audience)
- SEO: 4 new blog posts targeting mid-funnel keywords
- Webinar: "How Remote Teams Protect Focus Time" — live demo + Q&A
- Referral: launch referral program, email to all paid customers

### June — Optimise
- A/B test conversion emails based on April data
- Double down on best-performing ad creative from May
- Partner content: guest posts and co-marketing with Slack community

### Budget Allocation

| Channel | Q2 Budget % |
|---------|------------|
| LinkedIn Ads (Dana segment) | 35% |
| Content & SEO | 20% |
| Email (conversion + referral) | 15% |
| Webinar production | 15% |
| Retargeting | 10% |
| Referral program incentives | 5% |

- Lead with customer outcomes, not product features
- For Dana: speak to business impact and ROI, not just team happiness
- Still avoid: revolutionary, seamless, leverage, synergy
