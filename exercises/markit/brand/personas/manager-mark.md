## Role & Responsibilities

- **Title:** Engineering Manager / Senior Engineering Manager
- **Company:** Mid-size tech company, 100–500 employees, fully remote or hybrid
- **Team size:** 10–15 developers across 2–3 time zones
- **Reports to:** VP of Engineering or CTO

**Day-to-day responsibilities:**
- Running sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives
- Unblocking developers and removing obstacles
- Tracking team output and project timelines
- 1:1s with direct reports
- Coordinating with product, design, and QA
- Hiring and performance reviews

---

## Pain Points & Frustrations

1. **Meeting overload** — His calendar is a disaster. Back-to-back meetings leave no time for actual thinking or supporting his team.
2. **Fragmented focus time** — His developers keep getting pulled into Slack, impromptu meetings, and ad hoc requests right in the middle of deep work.
3. **No visibility without asking** — He doesn't know when his team is in focus mode vs. available, so he either interrupts them or stays in the dark.
4. **Time zone chaos** — Getting 10 developers across 3 time zones into the same rhythm is exhausting to coordinate manually.
5. **Guilt about productivity** — He worries his team isn't as productive as they could be, but doesn't want to micromanage to find out.

> "I spend half my day in meetings that could have been a Slack message, and the other half trying to figure out when my team is actually available."

## Current Solutions

|------|---------------|----------------|

| RescueTime | Personal time tracking | Individual only — no team coordination |
| Notion / Confluence | Project documentation | Not built for real-time coordination |
| Linear / Jira | Sprint and task tracking | No focus time awareness |

## Motivations & Goals

**Professional goals:**
- Ship high-quality products on time without burning out his team
- Build a reputation as a manager who protects his team's time
- Reduce churn — keep his best developers happy

**Personal goals:**
- Finish work at a reasonable hour
- Stop feeling like he's always behind
- Be proud of how he leads his team

**What success looks like to him:**
> "My team has protected focus blocks every day, meetings happen in defined windows, and I can see team availability at a glance — without pinging anyone."
