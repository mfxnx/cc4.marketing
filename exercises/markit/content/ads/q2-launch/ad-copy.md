## A/B Test Plan

| Test | Ad Set | Variable | Hypothesis |
|------|--------|----------|-----------|
| Test 1 | Mark retargeting | Pain point (1B) vs. social proof (1A) | Social proof will outperform for retargeting — they already know the problem |
| Test 2 | Dana new | "Book a Demo" vs. "Start Free Trial" CTA | Demo CTA will convert better for Dana — higher-touch buying process |
| Test 3 | Google Search | Set A vs. Set B headlines | Set B (intent-matched) will have higher CTR for bottom-funnel searches |
| Test 4 | Retargeting | Reminder (Ad 1) vs. Objection (Ad 2) | Ad 2 will convert better — objection handling for warm audience |

## Brand Voice Check

- ✓ No buzzwords — no "revolutionary", "seamless", "leverage"
- ✓ Benefits before features throughout
- ✓ Active voice in all copy
- ✓ Specific proof points used ("30%", "2+ hours", "5 minutes", "14-day")
- ✓ Dana copy speaks to business impact and ROI, not just team happiness
- ✓ Mark copy speaks to visibility and coordination, not micromanagement
